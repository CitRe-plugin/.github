# CitRe Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 23 August 2026**

CitRe is a privacy-first Chrome extension for creating, formatting, verifying, and exporting academic citations.

## Data CitRe processes

CitRe may process text that you explicitly select, highlight, copy, or paste for the purpose of creating a citation.

CitRe may also read citation-related metadata available on the current webpage, such as:

- article title
- author names
- journal or publication name
- publication year
- DOI
- volume and issue
- page range or article number
- canonical URL
- structured scholarly metadata exposed by the page

CitRe may store limited information locally in your browser, including your preferred citation style, Crossref preference, CitRe interface settings, pill visibility and position preferences, and your current citation draft.

## No background data collection

CitRe does **not** operate a developer-controlled data collection server and does not collect citation data in the background.

CitRe does not use:

- analytics or telemetry
- advertising or advertising trackers
- behavioral profiling
- browsing-history tracking
- data brokers
- sale of user data

The CitRe developer does not receive your highlighted text, citations, citation library, or browsing history through the extension.

## Local processing

Citation parsing, formatting, page-metadata extraction, and preference storage occur locally in your browser whenever possible.

## Crossref

Crossref integration is optional and is **disabled by default**.

If you explicitly enable Crossref functionality, CitRe may send citation-related information directly from your browser to the Crossref API at `https://api.crossref.org/`.

This may include information such as:

- DOI
- article title
- author names
- publication year
- journal name
- bibliographic text needed to identify or compare scholarly records

This information is used only to provide citation-related features such as:

- citation verification
- exact or similarity-based publication matching
- retrieval of canonical scholarly metadata
- related-article discovery

Crossref is an independent third party and its handling of requests is governed by Crossref's own privacy practices.

You may disable Crossref functionality at any time and continue using CitRe's local citation features.

## Clipboard access

CitRe may access the clipboard when necessary for a user-requested citation action.

This includes:

- importing text explicitly copied by the user
- supporting citation capture from Chrome's PDF viewer or other restricted contexts
- copying formatted citations
- copying citation metadata or export formats
- preparing citation data for reference-manager workflows

CitRe does not continuously monitor clipboard contents for unrelated purposes.

## Paperpile

When you choose **Save to Paperpile**, CitRe may prepare citation information such as a DOI or RIS record for transfer to Paperpile.

CitRe does not access or collect your Paperpile library, Paperpile account credentials, or unrelated Paperpile data.

Any subsequent interaction with Paperpile is controlled by the user and is subject to Paperpile's own privacy practices.

## Data sharing

CitRe does not sell user data.

Citation-related information is transferred to an external service only when necessary for a feature the user has deliberately enabled or invoked. The principal external service used by CitRe is Crossref for optional scholarly metadata lookup and verification.

CitRe does not transfer user data for:

- advertising
- marketing
- creditworthiness
- lending
- unrelated profiling
- sale to data brokers

## Data retention

CitRe does not maintain a developer-side database of user citation data.

Preferences and temporary citation information stored by the extension remain locally in Chrome.

Users may remove locally stored CitRe data by:

- using CitRe's **Clear** action for the current citation
- clearing extension data in Chrome
- uninstalling CitRe

## Personal information

CitRe does not request the user's name, postal address, account credentials, or other identity information.

Scholarly citation material may naturally contain the names of publication authors. CitRe processes those names only as bibliographic metadata and does not use them to identify or profile the CitRe user.

## Security

Where CitRe communicates with an external scholarly service such as Crossref, requests are made over HTTPS.

## Children

CitRe is a general-purpose academic citation tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if CitRe's functionality or data practices change.

The current version of this policy will remain publicly available in the CitRe repository and/or associated project website.

## Contact

For privacy questions or concerns about CitRe, please use the public contact method provided on the CitRe Chrome Web Store listing or the CitRe project repository.
