# Commercial Licensing

CitRe is available for non-commercial use under the terms described in the repository's `LICENSE.md`.

Commercial rights are available separately.

Organizations wishing to use CitRe commercially may request a bespoke commercial license for use cases including:

- inclusion in a commercial product or service;
- enterprise deployment;
- institutional or corporate distribution;
- OEM integration;
- white-label use;
- redistribution;
- commercial derivative works;
- strategic partnerships; or
- broader acquisition of commercial intellectual-property rights.

Commercial terms are negotiated individually and may include one-time licensing fees, recurring fees, royalties, exclusivity, field-of-use restrictions, or other mutually agreed terms.

No commercial rights are granted unless confirmed in a separate written agreement signed by the copyright holder.

For commercial licensing, partnership, or acquisition enquiries, please contact the project owner through the official CitRe repository or the contact information published on the CitRe Chrome Web Store listing.
